What's new in v21.4

  • The EPR note now pastes with bold headings. "Copy EPR note" copies a formatted version (bold, underlined section headings and bold labels) and a plain text version together. A note box that accepts formatting keeps the bold; a plain box gets the plain text, now split into sections with a blank line between them. If your EPR pastes the formatting badly, tick Copy the EPR note as plain text only in Settings. SBAR copies the same way.
  • The triage category is now near the top of the note, straight after the patient details, with the stream, reasons and next obs time. The note then runs: presenting complaint, observations and assessment, alerts, history, screening, plan.
  • Ambulance arrivals: Trolley is still filled in for you, but it now says it was assumed. Tapping Trolley confirms it (before, that tap cleared it). Switching back to Self clears an assumed Trolley.
  • "⚡ Quick" is now "⚡ Fast triage". It shows only what decides the category - arrival and patient, complaint, discriminator, pain, obs, allergies, infection control and the plan - and folds away the local ref, pre-hospital obs and treatment, Clinical Frailty Scale, PMH, medicines and high-risk groups, child analgesia and safeguarding, and the screening questions. Each folded card has its own "Show ..." button. Anything still missing from a folded section is listed under "Fast triage - for later", and the note says fast triage was used.
  • Blank no longer means "normal". "No IPC flags", high-risk medicine groups "None of these" and (for children) "No safeguarding concerns" are now buttons you tap. Until you tick something or tap the button, the note says NOT SCREENED / NOT CHECKED and it shows in "Still to record". The #NOF and corridor care lists are things done, so blank there still reads "none recorded".
  • Fixes: choosing Male now clears a ticked pregnancy box (it was hidden but still switched the score to MEOWS). If the age and the flowchart don't match (an adult flowchart for a child, or the other way round), "Still to record" now asks you to check the flowchart.
  • Sepsis in under 5s now follows NICE NG254 too. The sepsis panel uses NG254 table 1 for children under 5: breathing rate and heart rate by age band (under 1, 1 to 2, 3 to 4), SpO2 in air, capillary refill and temperature are scored from the obs (under 36°C is high risk; 38°C or more under 3 months is high risk; 39°C or more at 3 to 6 months is moderate to high), with tick boxes for behaviour, cry, grunting, apnoea, skin and the rest. ACVPU P or U counts as "does not wake". Under 3 months, the panel adds NICE's advice on parenteral antibiotics for fever. The fever traffic light (NG143) still shows in the Paediatric card.
  • Provisional NEWS2 from the obs you have. As soon as one obs is in, you see "Provisional NEWS2 at least X (n of 7 obs)" with the risk it already shows (for example "Medium risk or higher"), in the obs section, the decision panel and the bar pinned at the top on phones. Missing obs are listed and are not counted as 0, so the full score can only be the same or higher. A low provisional score never says "low risk" and never sets the next obs hours away - it asks you to finish the obs.
  • Fewer taps: Sex and Mobility are one-tap buttons instead of drop-downs (tap again to clear). Choosing Air or O₂ jumps straight to the blood pressure. On a phone the keyboard's Next key moves through the obs. On a keyboard, type the obs and press Enter between them; at Air/O₂ press A or O, and at ACVPU press the letter - both move straight on.
  • Sepsis in ages 5 to 15 now follows NICE NG254 (v21.1). Answer "Could this be an infection?", and the NG254 high and moderate-to-high risk criteria for the child's age are scored from the obs, with tick boxes for the rest. High risk and 2 or more moderate-to-high criteria bring blood gas, cultures, paediatric sepsis bloods and IV access into the plan.
  • More ECG triggers: chest pain on any flowchart, shortness of breath, a new oxygen requirement and a significant cardiac history in the PMH (adults).
  • Blood gas has a low threshold: it is now suggested wherever lactate, glucose, potassium, sodium, pH or CO2 would help - for example abdominal pain, overdose, diarrhoea and vomiting, confusion, reduced consciousness, NEWS2 of 3 or more, new oxygen need and abnormal temperature.
  • New investigations and cannula engine. The plan now reacts to everything you record, not just the complaint: obs, NEWS2, sepsis risk, BM and ketones, consciousness, pregnancy, high-risk medicines and the head injury, stroke and fever tools. Each suggestion shows why it fired and its source.
  • "Do now" marks time-critical tests (for example ECG within 10 minutes for chest pain, blood cultures and gas for high-risk sepsis, BM when consciousness is reduced).
  • Cannula decision: Insert now / Recommended / Consider / Not needed now, with the reasons, a suggested size and cautions (fistula arm, injured limb, children). A cannula is only advised when IV treatment is likely soon (sepsis, shock, bleeding, hypoxia, severe asthma, fast arrhythmia, DKA, stroke within 24 h, antidotes, hip fracture). Stable chest pain, confusion, collapse and overdose get their bloods by venepuncture instead of an idle cannula.
  • "Neck breather" is now "Altered airway (tracheostomy / laryngectomy)", with the reminder to oxygenate the stoma.
  • Ketones box next to BM: ketones of 3.0 or more with a high BM flags possible DKA.
  • Over 65s: no urine dipstick to look for infection (UKHSA) - the plan offers a urine culture only if a UTI is suspected.
  • Pregnancy test becomes "Do now" for abdominal pain, PV bleeding, collapse, urinary or gut symptoms in anyone who could be pregnant (NICE NG126).
  • Decision panel stays on screen: category, "see by" time, NEWS2, sepsis and anything still missing.
  • Nothing is assumed. Pain, consciousness, oxygen and mobility start as "not recorded"; patients show NOT YET TRIAGED until you choose a discriminator.
  • A full NEWS2 needs all 7 observations; until then it is shown as provisional. SpO₂ Scale 2 follows the RCP chart exactly, and ACVPU includes new confusion.
  • Sepsis follows NICE NG253: answer "Could this be an infection?" first, then risk comes from NEWS2.
  • Pain follows the MTS ruler (5–7 moderate = Yellow, 8–10 severe = Orange). Orange target is 10 minutes.
  • New nurse tools: NICE head injury criteria (tells you when to tell the ED doctor), NICE fever traffic light (under 5s), ROSIER and time last known well, ECG-in-10-minutes, 4AT, mental health risk, infection control, corridor care, children's safeguarding.
  • Keyboard: press ? for shortcuts. Complaint search understands "cp", "sob", "dib", "od", "fall", etc.

Local MEOWS and PEWS are still the app's own versions, not the national MEWS/PEWS charts - use your trust chart where they differ.
